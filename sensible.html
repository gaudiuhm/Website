Sensible

Real-time awareness of your environment so you can stay calm and in control.

Date March 2026
Role Solo Designer
Tools Figma
Duration 4 Days
The Problem

Humans experience sensory overload when sound, light, and environmental stimuli exceed what the brain can comfortably process. But there is currently no way to quantify this experience or detect it before it becomes overwhelming.

01

Sensory overload is common. Millions experience overwhelm from noise, light, crowds, and constant stimulation.

02

Environments are becoming more intense. Offices, schools, and public facilities expose us to continuous sensory input.

03

Overload is difficult to detect early. People only realize they're overwhelmed once stress or anxiety has already begun.

04

There is no way to measure sensory load. Unlike heart rate or sleep, sensory stimulation has no clear metric.

The Opportunity

What if we could measure sensory environments the same way we measure heart rate?

A new measurable signal: Sensory Overload Score

A simple metric that quantifies the sensory load of an environment and alerts users before they reach their threshold.

The Solution

A real-time sensory scoring system that helps users anticipate overload.

The system monitors environmental stimuli and physiological signals continuously, surfacing a simple metric before the user reaches their threshold.

  1. Light
  2. Sound
  3. Crowd Density
  4. Heart Rate
Use Cases

Who benefits from Sensible?

Student in a Lecture Hall

A student enters a crowded lecture hall where overlapping conversations, bright lighting, and movement quickly raise sensory stimulation. Sensible detects the rising sensory load and alerts the student before it becomes overwhelming. The app recommends moving to a quieter seat or activating Calm Mode to reduce distractions.

Commuter on the Subway

During a busy subway commute, noise spikes, crowd density, and motion contribute to sensory stress. Sensible continuously monitors these inputs and predicts when sensory overload may occur. The user receives a warning and suggestions like stepping off for a break or enabling noise reduction.

Office Worker

An employee working in an open office experiences fluctuating stimulation from conversations, screens, and movement throughout the day. Sensible identifies patterns in their environment and highlights peak sensory stress periods. With these insights, the employee can schedule breaks or move to quieter workspace to maintain focus.

The Future

Sensory tracking as the next frontier of wellness.

As technology continues to integrate with our daily lives, tools that understand and support human sensory experiences will become increasingly important. Future systems may be able to detect, predict, and adapt to our environments in real time, helping individuals maintain focus, comfort, and wellbeing in an increasingly stimulating world.

Environments that adapt to people. Smart buildings, classrooms, and workplaces could automatically adjust sensory stimuli based on the user.

Personal sensory awareness. Future wearable devices may allow individuals to understand how their bodies react to different environments.

A new dimension of wellness. Just as fitness trackers helped us monitor physical health, sensory tracking may become an essential tool for maintaining mental and emotional balance.
